# Branham Hills Football Pool: 40-Year Commish Lore & Authentic Email Vault (1986–2026)

## 1. Executive Heritage & 40-Year Foundations

The **Branham Hills Football Pool** represents four decades of straight-up gridiron picking, neighborhood camaraderie, and sharp-witted banter. Founded in 1986 by **Don "The Commish" Threet** out of the San Jose Little League coaching and volunteer umpiring community—alongside co-founders Lanny Ross, Rudy Perez, Keith Sorensen, Richard, and Robert—the pool began on yellow legal pads and carbon-copy pick sheets distributed across local dugouts and Mountain Mike's Pizza parlors. Over forty consecutive NFL seasons, technology evolved from office fax machines and landlines to digital vaults and web applications, but the foundational principle remained completely unchanged: straight-up wins, no point spreads, and absolute authority rested in the hands of Commissioner Emeritus Don Threet.

With Don stepping into the role of **The Consultant** and his son Joel Threet assuming day-to-day operations as **The Admin**, the title of "The Commissioner" was officially retired in honor of Don's forty years of leadership. This vault aggregates the authentic, unedited historical dispatches, email logs, weekly standing recaps, and legendary quotes extracted directly from Don Threet's communications spanning 2009 through 2021.

## 3. Chronological Season Email Archives & Dispatch Logs (2011–2021)

Reviewing the recovered historic email archives reveals the week-by-week evolution of the pool across a decade of active league communication.

### 2011 Season: The Year of "Rudymania"
The 2011 season stands in league lore as the most dominant individual performance ever recorded. Co-founder Rudy Perez accomplished the impossible by holding the #1 position on the master leaderboard for seventeen consecutive weeks.

> *"2011 will be forever known as Rudymania; Won Wire to Wire! 17 weeks in a row!"*  
> — **Don "The Commish" Threet, 2011 Master Ledger Certification**

### 2012 Season: "Jessepaloosa" & Back-to-Back Championships

Everyone picked SF but Keith, even Richard (die hard Rams fan) and Nick (49er hater for years and years and a Cheese Head)... no accounting for taste. Jeff, last week's winner only got 7, son of mine, you gotta catch your sister."*

* **Week 9 Integrity Defense (November 6, 2012):** When pool members joked at Monday Night Football at Mountain Mike's about Don correcting Jessica's pick sheet, Don forwarded Jessica's original timestamped email (Sun Nov 4, 8:50 AM) to the entire league:

## 4. League Lore & Legendary Characters

### The Legend of Elvis (The Vegas Favorite Bot)
To maintain an even number of pick sheets and honor pool tradition, **Elvis Presley** exists as a permanent automated contender. Sponsored by Rudy Perez, Elvis's picks are automatically populated based on Vegas point-spread favorites. When Rudy used to submit identical picks for himself and Elvis, Don laid down the law: *"Rudy, you gotta give Elvis different picks, otherwise it's just a wash!"* Over forty years, Elvis has frequently finished ahead of veteran human contenders and won multiple weekly crowns.

### Juan "Mr. Football" Vargas & The Yellow Jacket
Juan Vargas earned immortal pool status during the 2018 season by maintaining an iron grip on 1st place from Week 2 through Week 17. His dominance led Don Threet to issue an official mandate that all pool members address Juan exclusively as "Mr. Football." The tradition culminated in the presentation of a green and yellow championship jacket during the annual playoff banquet.

### The 3rd Generation: Jordan & Trevor
Carrying the Threet family football tradition into its third generation, Don's grandsons **Jordan** and **Trevor** became major forces in the standings. Their milestones include Jordan's perfect 16-for-16 Week 15 performance in 2017 and strong finishes throughout the 40th Anniversary season, evoking Don's famous refrain: *"Ain't my grandsons GRAND!"*

## 5. The Official Code & Fine Enforcement Ledger

### Don's Code (The Four Golden Rules)

1. **Straight-Up Winners Only:** Every game, every week. Contenders pick outright winners with zero point spreads.
2. **Monday Night Combined Points Tiebreaker:** Predict the total combined points scored by both teams in the Monday Night Football game. The tiebreaker applies strictly to break a tie for 1st place rank for the week.
3. **Mascot Prohibition:** Picks must list City, State, or Region only (e.g., "LA Rams" or "LA Chargers"). Listing mascots (e.g., "Rams" or "Chiefs") violates the Commish's cardinal rule (*"DO NOT USE MASCOTS! City, State, or Region only....I have spoken!"*).
4. **The Umpire Penalty Jar:** Operated strictly for fun and bragging rights (zero monetary value), Penalty Flags are assessed for:
   * Late pick submissions or missing deadlines.
   * Using team mascots instead of city/place names.
   * Arguing with the Commissioner or questioning officiating calls (*"Clarification: Any OBJECTION earns a Penalty Flag in the Umpire Jar! I have spoken!"*).
   * Finishing in cellar duty on the weekly leaderboard.
